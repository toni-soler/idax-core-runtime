[IDAX Core Runtime Binary License 1.0](IDAX_CORE_BINARY_LICENSE.md). It does not
grant access to source code, source JARs, Javadoc JARs, generators or internal

The public Maven endpoint permits anonymous downloads. Standalone mirroring and
redistribution remain prohibited by the binary license. A JAR can be decompiled;
the open-core boundary is legal and contractual, not a technical secrecy
guarantee.
